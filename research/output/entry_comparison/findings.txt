Saved-plan entry comparison — 2026-10-30

Scope: 24 planned legs across nine trading sessions, October 8–30, 2026.
The November 2 plan was excluded. Every leg has continuous minute data from
09:15 through the planned 15:10 exit. candles.json preserves the input data;
summary.json records candle and plan hashes and the exact experimental rules.

Frozen experiments (no parameter search):
1. Fixed limit: original entry, stop and first target.
2. Confirmed fixed: completed five-minute directional candle beyond the planned
   entry and session VWAP; enter at the next minute open, retain original exits.
3. Opening range: completed five-minute close beyond the first five-minute
   range and session VWAP; enter next minute open, reset stop to the opposite
   range edge with a 0.5% minimum distance, retain the plan's reward/risk ratio.
Both confirmation variants stop searching at 10:30. One attempt per leg.

Each variant uses the same maximum planned rupee risk and notional exposure
per leg. Actual deployed risk can be lower after whole-share sizing. Short
and long trades use their correct buy/sell sides for charges. No capital is
reallocated between legs. These sums are NOT account returns.

Net results with estimated fees and 0.05% slippage per side:
                           Resolved trades   Net winners   Net P&L
Fixed limit                       11              4        +Rs 820.68
Confirmed fixed                   10              4        -Rs 1,100.77
Opening range                     11              5        -Rs 580.26

At 0.10% slippage per side:
Fixed limit: +Rs 118.80; confirmed fixed: -Rs 1,397.79;
opening range: -Rs 796.51.

Fixed-limit exclusions: eight invalid stop/target configurations at attempted
fill, four unfilled entries, and one ambiguous entry bar. Confirmation changes
which legs trade; the resolved subsets are not identical. Skipped legs use no
capital and earn zero. Ambiguous outcomes remain unknown and are not in P&L.

The fixed-limit result without its strongest trade (SHANTIGOLD, October 12)
is -Rs 918.52. This concentration weakens the apparent positive result.

The earlier Rs 1,011.60 replay is not directly comparable: it retained original
quantities and favorable target gap fills. This controlled comparison caps
actual stop risk and notional exposure and caps target fills at the target.

Conclusion: neither tested morning-entry alternative improved the sample.
This does not reject all morning confirmation rules or establish a profitable
baseline. The data was already inspected, the sample is small, and the methods
do not simulate live guards, partial exits, latency, order queues or portfolio
constraints. Fixed future rules need independent historical and forward tests.

Next research priority: correct indicator/backtest measurement, then compare
stock-selection methods using one frozen entry/exit rule on independent data.
Do not tune additional entry rules against these same 24 legs.

Verification: focused synthetic checks covered intrabar stops, invalid levels,
ambiguous bars, missing minutes, next-bar execution, risk sizing, opening-range
levels, square-off timing and increased slippage costs. No live orders placed.

Cost source, checked 2026-10-30: https://zerodha.com/charges/
Brokerage/taxes are schedule estimates; slippage is an assumption, not a
measurement. Contract-note rounding and all incidental charges are not modeled.
